QR Code Generator

showcase

QR Code Generator is an App I developed on a teamfor my university to use. It came to our attention that there were a lot of qr codes being generated on campus each week. These qr codes were initially created using a variety of tools. With an organization so big it seemed to be better to have a universal place for employees to generate their qr codes

This app uses a LAMP stack with Angular JS, HTML, and CSS on the front end, and PHP running in the back end. It has a variety of colors and pictures that can easily be attaached to the qr code. Overall the universtiy uses this on a regular basis still and it proved to be a valuable addition to the schools app collection
