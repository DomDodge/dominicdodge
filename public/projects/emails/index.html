Email Template Generator

showcase

Email Template Generator is an App I co-developed for my university to use. The app gives the University an in-house alternative to the many paid email template generators out there. It offers a variety of custom styles and decorations that make each email feel both unique and in line with Utah Tech University's branding.

The UI provides a tool set for the users to create the email exactly how they want. They can create new blocks with unique coloring, font, sizing, images, and more. The customization of emails works similarly to WordPress and other CMS software. In the end, users can copy the shortcode over and paste it right in there inbox for a nice stylized email.
